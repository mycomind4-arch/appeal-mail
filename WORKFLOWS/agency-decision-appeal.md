# Agency Decision Appeal — Gold Workflow

This workflow analyzes an agency decision and prepares a human-reviewed appeal response.

## Authority model
Agency and jurisdiction are resolved before consequential procedural conclusions. The decision notice and current authoritative sources are the controlling record. Unsupported deadlines, recipients, forms, filing destinations, hearing rights, exhaustion requirements, eligibility rules, facts, and outcomes remain unresolved.

## Pipeline
Upload → classify/extract → authority resolution → deadline analysis → evidence/contradiction/timeline analysis → strategy → Gemini draft → independent Gemini validation → human approval → custom packet pricing → Stripe → deterministic PDF → MailMyPDF → provider submission → tracking/proof.
