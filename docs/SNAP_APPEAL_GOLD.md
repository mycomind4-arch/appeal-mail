## Authority
SNAP is federally governed but administered through state agencies. The workflow must keep federal USDA/FNS requirements distinct from state-specific hearing procedures. The notice is the primary case-specific procedural source; current authoritative federal and state sources are used for verification. ## Required behavior
- Never invent deadlines, hearing forums, filing methods, recipients, forms, eligibility facts, or outcomes.
- Extract notice facts before making procedural conclusions.
- Flag unsupported household, income, resource, verification, immigration, and eligibility assertions.
- Keep state-procedure questions unresolved until verified.
- Require independent AI validation and human approval before paid mailing.
