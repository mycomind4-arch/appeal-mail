# Social Security Reconsideration — Supreme Authority Gold Standard

## Authority
SSA reconsideration is source-controlled. The workflow uses the actual notice plus current SSA pages and applicable regulations. SSA currently states that disability and non-medical reconsideration requests generally must be submitted within 60 days after receipt of the decision, while regulations provide for extensions when good cause is shown. The workflow must calculate and display this from authoritative sources and the notice, never from a generic date assumption.

## Safety
Never invent medical or vocational facts, eligibility, deadlines, forms, recipient instructions, or outcomes. Keep administrative reconsideration distinct from later hearing, Appeals Council review, and federal court review.
