# VA Claim Appeal — Gold Standard

## Authority

The workflow uses the VA decision letter plus current official VA decision-review guidance. The three principal review lanes are Supplemental Claim, Higher-Level Review, and Board Appeal. Higher-Level Review does not consider new evidence; Supplemental Claims use new and relevant evidence; Board evidence rules depend on the selected lane.

## Pipeline

Upload → source-grounded Gemini analysis → review-lane mapping → evidence/contradiction analysis → Gemini drafting → independent Gemini validation → human approval → packet pricing → Stripe → deterministic response PDF → MailMyPDF upload → provider submission → tracking/proof.

## Safety

The workflow does not invent service history, medical facts, ratings, effective dates, deadlines, forms, recipients, eligibility, filing rules, review-lane eligibility, or outcomes. Unsupported conclusions remain unresolved until grounded in the notice or authoritative VA material.
